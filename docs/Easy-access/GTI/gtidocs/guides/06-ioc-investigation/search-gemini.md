# ask-questions-with-gemini

To simplify search, Gemini in Threat Intelligence redefines search with natural language. With this feature, you can ask a natural language question and quickly get AI-powered overviews of a topic.

When you perform a single term search, such as, "what is APT44", you get the Gemini search summary.

> ⚠️
>
> Gemini in Threat Intelligence search currently only generates search summaries based on the Google Threat Intelligence reports, threat actor, malware, and campaign data.
>
> Note: the Gemini summary is only displayed when there is information from the source data to answer the search query.

## suggested-search-queries

| **Use case** | **Suggested queries** |
| --- | --- |
| Get an overview of threat actors | "What is lockbit?" <br/>"What is Scattered Spider?" |
| Deep dive into specific questions | "What is the log4j vulnerability aka Log4Shell?" <br/>"What are the indicators of killnet?" <br/>"How do I detect alphv?" <br/>"What industries does APT29 target?" |

> 📘
>
> Tips on how to phrase your queries to get the most useful results:
>
> * Phrase queries in a question format.
> * Asking specific questions such as "how does APT29 move laterally?" typically generates better responses.
> * Keep questions concise and to the point. Avoid overly complex sentences or multiple questions in a single query.
