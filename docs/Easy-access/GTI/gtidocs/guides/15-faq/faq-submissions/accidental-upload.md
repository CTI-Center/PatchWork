# i-accidentally-uploaded-a-file-with-confidential-or-sensitive-information-to-google-ti,-can-you-please-delete-it?

We are very concerned about the privacy of our users and will do everything that is in our hands in order to ensure that privacy is preserved, please use our [contact form](https://www.virustotal.com/gui/contact-us/private-upload) to inform us about the issue. Please note that to provide effective assistance, specific and detailed information about the sensitive information exposed is crucial.

We also recommend to read our [privacy policy](https://cloud.google.com/terms/secops/privacy-notice) and [terms of service](https://cloud.google.com/terms/secops?hl=en).
